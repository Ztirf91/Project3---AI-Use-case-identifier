# Future go-to-market sprints

The MVP produces a full pre-meeting brief for an AI consultant (thinking aid for first conversations). The sprints below first prove that brief in real meetings, then turn it into a paid entry offer, and only later put a lighter public report on the web as a lead magnet. Companies get that light report in exchange for email and phone — not a product sold to other consultants or agencies. Each sprint is two weeks.

## Sprint 1: Thinking aid in real first meetings

1. **Goal:** Learn whether the full brief makes first conversations sharper — less guessing about the business, clearer next steps on use cases.
2. **Target user / buyer:** Owners and managing directors of German SMEs (about 20 to 500 employees) reached through the consultant's existing network and warm contacts, especially manufacturing, wholesale, and e-commerce.
3. **Channel or motion:** Eight to ten discovery calls prepared with the brief. After each call, log which assumptions were confirmed or wrong and which of the three use cases the client found most relevant. Feed corrections into prompts and the use-case library.
4. **Key deliverable:** A call log template, an accuracy score per report section, and a revised rubric and library based on what stuck in the room.
5. **Success metric:** In at least 7 of 10 calls, the client confirms or rejects at least one of the three use cases with a reason, and at least 3 calls lead to a proposal for a workshop or pilot.

## Sprint 2: From conversation to a paid entry offer

1. **Goal:** Turn the validated brief into the entry point of a paid offer in one industry where the library is strongest.
2. **Target user / buyer:** Mid-sized manufacturers with a dealer network (bicycles, machinery, building products).
3. **Channel or motion:** A fixed-price "AI use case workshop" (half a day, on site), sold with a short preview from the brief. Outreach to about 50 target companies with a personalized preview, followed by a call.
4. **Key deliverable:** An industry-specific library pack (around 25 cards), a workshop agenda built on the three use cases, and a one-page offer.
5. **Success metric:** Two paid workshops booked, and a reply rate of at least 10 percent on the outreach.

## Sprint 3: Web lead magnet with a light report

1. **Goal:** Prove that SME decision-makers will trade email and phone number for a company-specific, public-facing report — a lighter cut of the consultant brief (three use cases and a short process overview; without the full internal meeting-prep framing).
2. **Target user / buyer:** Owners and managing directors of the same SME segments who find the offer online themselves (not other consultants or agencies).
3. **Channel or motion:** A one-page form (company URL, name, email, phone). The form triggers the graph through a webhook and delivers the light report. Promoted through LinkedIn posts that show one anonymized sample, plus regional business networks where useful.
4. **Key deliverable:** Landing page, public report variant (light template), consent text, webhook trigger, delivery, and leads written to a simple CRM sheet / `lead.json`.
5. **Success metric:** 25 completed requests, and at least 20 percent reachable for a follow-up call on the phone number they gave.
