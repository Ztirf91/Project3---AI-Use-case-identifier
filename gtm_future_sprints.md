# Future go-to-market sprints

The MVP produces a pre-meeting brief for an AI consultant. The sprints below turn it into a lead engine and then a paid offer for German SMEs. Each sprint is two weeks.

## Sprint 1: Brief for an email address

1. **Goal:** Prove that SME decision-makers will trade contact details for a company-specific AI brief.
2. **Target user / buyer:** Owners and managing directors of German companies with 20 to 500 employees in manufacturing, wholesale, and e-commerce.
3. **Channel or motion:** A one-page form (company URL, name, email). The form triggers the graph through a webhook and emails the report within 15 minutes. Promoted through the consultant's LinkedIn posts that show one anonymized brief, plus two regional business networks (IHK events, Mittelstand groups).
4. **Key deliverable:** Landing page, webhook trigger, email delivery, consent text, and `lead.json` written to a simple CRM sheet.
5. **Success metric:** 30 completed requests, and at least 25 percent book a 30-minute follow-up call.

## Sprint 2: Validate the questions in real calls

1. **Goal:** Find out whether the "questions for the first meeting" actually move a conversation toward a decision.
2. **Target user / buyer:** The leads from Sprint 1 who booked a call.
3. **Channel or motion:** Ten discovery calls run from the brief. After each call, record which assumptions were confirmed or wrong and which use case the client found most relevant. Feed corrections back into the prompts and the use-case library.
4. **Key deliverable:** A call log template, an accuracy score per report section, and a revised rubric and library (target: 40 verified cards).
5. **Success metric:** In at least 7 of 10 calls, the client confirms or rejects at least one of the three use cases with a reason, and at least 3 calls lead to a proposal for a workshop.

## Sprint 3: One vertical pack, sold as a workshop

1. **Goal:** Turn the brief into the entry point of a paid offer in one industry where the library is strongest.
2. **Target user / buyer:** Mid-sized manufacturers with a dealer network (bicycles, machinery, building products).
3. **Channel or motion:** A fixed-price "AI use case workshop" (half a day, on site), sold with the brief as a free preview. Outreach to 50 target companies with a personalized brief attached, followed by a call.
4. **Key deliverable:** An industry-specific library pack (around 25 cards), a workshop agenda built on the three use cases, and a one-page offer.
5. **Success metric:** Two paid workshops booked, and a reply rate of at least 10 percent on the outreach.
