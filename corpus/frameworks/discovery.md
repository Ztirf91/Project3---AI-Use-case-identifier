# What an AI consultant needs to find out

General discovery — independent of any specific use case. Own words for the brief.

## Stance

As an AI consultant, start from the business, not from the tool: what the company wants to achieve, what blocks that, and which knowledge it can put to work — how it earns money, where cost and errors pile up, how work really runs, and where useful data and experience live (systems, files, or people’s heads). Only then ask where AI can connect information, create options, or take on steps — and which measurable result would prove the change was worth it.

## Questions for the first conversation

1. What would “success in 12 months” look like in one sentence?
2. Where do cost, delays, rework or complaints hurt most — and who owns that?
3. Where does critical know-how live today, and how hard is it to find and trust?
4. Who would own a first pilot, and which one metric would convince leadership?
